Terms of Use

Last updated: 20 September 2026. Questions: crkvaa@gmail.com.

Flugtickets Marketplace helps members discover and list flight tickets. The service is not an airline, payment escrow or guarantor of a ticket. Before listing or purchasing, confirm that the airline permits the necessary name change or transfer and agree directly on transfer and payment terms.

List only tickets you have the right to transfer. Do not post misleading, illegal, abusive or sexually explicit content or expose booking references, QR codes or another person's private information. Chat images appear to the recipient immediately. Members may report content and block users; administrators may review reports, hide content or restrict access.

A one-time publishing fee is charged per listing regardless of whether the ticket sells. The exact price is shown in the app before purchase. This is a listing fee, not a payment for the ticket itself. An unpaid draft is not publicly visible. Sellers can close published listings, while deletion of unpaid drafts is allowed within the app.

Members arrange ticket payment and transfer directly. Take reasonable precautions; an image of a ticket alone does not prove validity. For support contact the email above.

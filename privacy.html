Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 September 2026. For privacy requests contact crkvaa@gmail.com.

Data we use

When you sign up, we collect your email, name, phone number, authentication details and verification status. Listings include flight, price and seller details. Chats may contain messages and images you choose to send. We process reports, blocks, listing views and payment confirmation status to operate and protect the marketplace.

Why and with whom

We use this data for account access, listings, buyer–seller communication, abuse prevention and support. Supabase provides authentication, database and private image storage. Apple processes the in-app payment of the publishing fee; Flugtickets Marketplace does not receive complete payment details. A seller and the other participant in a chat can see the information shared with them. Images appear to the recipient immediately; reported images may be hidden pending review by an administrator.

Retention and choices

Your listings and conversations remain until removed under the service rules or your account is deleted. In Profile you can permanently delete your account; this also removes your listings and shared conversations, including images, for participants. Some transaction records may remain with a payment provider as legally required. Contact the address above to request access or correction of your data.

Safety

Do not send QR codes, booking references or personal data in ticket images. You can report messages or members and block contacts within the app.
